Privacy Policy

This page explains how Al Rashed Cement Driver Application handles operational data, location visibility, trip documents, and device information.

App Purpose

This application is intended only for authorized drivers and operational users of Al Rashed Cement.

Data Collection

The application may collect operational information required to execute, monitor, and validate logistics trips.

Camera Images

Used when capturing required trip documents, delivery notes, identity documents, signatures, or evidence related to trip execution.

Location Data

Used during active trips for tracking, operational visibility, route monitoring, and delivery validation when enabled.

Device Identifiers

Used for secure sessions, device authorization, audit review, and account protection.

Data Usage

Purpose of Data

Data is used only to provide logistics functionality, driver trip execution, operational monitoring, document validation, reporting, and integration with company backend systems.

Data Sharing

Data may be shared with authorized company systems strictly for operational, audit, security, and logistics management purposes.

Important Note

Collected data is not sold to third parties. Data is processed only to support authorized logistics operations and secured application access.

Data Security

Access to application data is restricted to authorized users and protected by authentication, authorization, audit controls, and server-side security rules.

Contact Information

For privacy requests, questions, or concerns about this policy, contact the authorized IT support team.
